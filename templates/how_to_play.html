HOW TO PLAY QUIZ CLASH

Master the rules of engagement, speed mechanics, and strategic streaks to conquer the multiplayer arena.

1

Accessing the Arena

You can create a permanent account with unique battle tags and customizable avatars, or jump straight into the action with 1-Click Guest Mode for instant presentations and testing.

Full Profile Tracking Instant Guest Access
2

Connecting Opponents in Lobbies

Host a room to receive a unique 6-character room code (e.g. QC-4821). Share this code with your rival, classmate, or open an Incognito window. When both players click READY UP, the host launches the synchronized match.

3

Synchronized Countdown & Single Lockout

Both players view identical questions and options simultaneously. You have 15 seconds (or your host-configured timer). Once you click an option, your answer is permanently locked to prevent cheating or rapid guessing.

4

Answer Reveal, Explanations & Victory

When both players answer (or the clock runs out), the server reveals the true answer, explanations, and awards points. At the end of the round, the winner is celebrated with sound fanfare, confetti, and leaderboard points!
